Anthropic Company Overview

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, along with several 
other former OpenAI researchers. The company is headquartered in San Francisco, California.

Anthropic's primary product is Claude, an AI assistant built on large language model 
technology. Claude is designed with a focus on safety, helpfulness, and honesty. The 
company uses a technique called Constitutional AI to train Claude to be helpful while 
avoiding harmful outputs.

Anthropic has raised significant funding, including a $300 million Series B round in 2023 
and a $4 billion investment from Amazon. The company's valuation reached approximately 
$15 billion by late 2023.

The company employs approximately 500 people as of 2024 and focuses heavily on AI safety 
research alongside its commercial products. Anthropic publishes research on topics 
including interpretability, alignment, and the societal impacts of AI systems.

Claude is available through Anthropic's API and through claude.ai, a consumer-facing 
web interface. Enterprise customers can access Claude through AWS Bedrock and Google 
Cloud Vertex AI.
